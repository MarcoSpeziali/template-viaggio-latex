\tripday{Friday 12 May}{Genoa}{Arrival}
We reach Brignole mid-morning and head straight for the old port. The first
thought is focaccia, still warm, eaten standing on the quay.

\sight{The caruggi}
Narrow alleys, washing hung out to dry, shops that seem to have stopped a century
ago. We get lost on purpose and find the way back by following the smell of the sea.

\photogrid[2]{

\sight{The Lanterna}
The tallest lighthouse in the Mediterranean, seen at sunset from the pier.

\photo{photos/2028-05-12/lanterna}{The Lanterna at sunset}

\memory{4}

\tripday{Saturday 13 May}{Camogli}{The painted houses}
A regional train to Camogli, then the path to San Fruttuoso: two hours among holm
oaks and glimpses of the sea, and the abbey appearing out of nowhere.
